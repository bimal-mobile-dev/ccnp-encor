# [Domain-4](#domain-4-network-assurance) **Network Assurance**

- **10% weighting.** Monitoring, verifying, and troubleshooting network health at an enterprise scale — knowing the network is working, not just that it's configured

## Diagnostic tools and troubleshooting

- **Enterprise-scale troubleshooting**: applying structured diagnostic methodology to complex, multi-device enterprise topologies, extending CCNA's troubleshooting foundation to scenarios with many more variables (multi-area OSPF, redundant paths, multiple VLANs)

## Monitoring protocols

- **SNMP and streaming telemetry**: comparing traditional polling-based SNMP monitoring with modern, more efficient streaming telemetry approaches for real-time network visibility
- **NetFlow**: understanding traffic flow analysis for identifying usage patterns, bandwidth consumption, and anomalies across the enterprise network

## Cisco Catalyst Center (formerly DNA Center)

- **Applying network configuration and assurance through Catalyst Center**: Cisco's centralized network management and assurance platform — v1.2 explicitly renamed this from "DNA Center" throughout the blueprint, and candidates should recognize both names may appear in older materials or real-world documentation still catching up to the rename
- Understanding how centralized assurance platforms like Catalyst Center provide network-wide visibility that individual device-by-device monitoring can't match efficiently at enterprise scale
