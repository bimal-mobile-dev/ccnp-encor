- **15% weighting.** Enterprise-scale network design — the strategic layer above CCNA's device-level configuration focus

## Enterprise network design principles

- **Design methodologies**: hierarchical design principles (core/distribution/access, or collapsed two-tier) applied at enterprise scale, extending CCNA's introductory topology coverage into full design trade-off analysis (scalability, resiliency, cost)
- **High availability mechanisms**: redundancy protocols and design patterns that keep an enterprise network running through component failure — the design-level counterpart to CCNA's device-level FHRP configuration

## Wireless architecture models

- **Autonomous vs. centralized (WLC-based) wireless architectures**: comparing deployment models at a design level, building on CCNA's introductory wireless architecture concepts
- **Wireless roaming and RF design considerations**: enterprise-scale wireless deployment factors not relevant at CCNA's smaller-scale scope

## SD-WAN and SD-Access

- **Cisco SD-WAN**: understanding the components and design considerations of Cisco's software-defined WAN solution — centralized policy control over distributed WAN connections
- **Cisco SD-Access**: fabric-based campus network architecture that separates the network's underlying physical topology from its logical policy — a significant architectural shift from traditional VLAN-based campus design, and one of ENCOR's most conceptually dense topics
